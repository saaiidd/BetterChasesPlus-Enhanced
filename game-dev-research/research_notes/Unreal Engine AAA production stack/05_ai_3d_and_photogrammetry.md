# Specialist 05: AI-Generated 3D Assets, AI Concept Art, and Photogrammetry (state as of 2026-10-09)

Scope: AI 3D generators (hosted and open weights), photogrammetry and Gaussian splatting, AI 2D tools for concept art and textures, their API/MCP support, licensing for a commercial Steam release, and the practical workflows that turn their output into playable UE5 assets. Overlaps with specialists 01 (marketplaces, Megascans), 02 (characters), 04 (Blender/Substance retopology and texturing), 09 (Nanite, performance), 11 (MCP automation) and 12 (licensing depth, Steam) are mentioned only briefly.

Method and caveats:
- About 37 searches/fetches. Official pricing pages, terms, license files, GitHub repos and vendor docs were preferred. Vendor blogs, SEO "comparison" sites and affiliate review sites are explicitly marked Low confidence. No accounts were created, nothing was purchased, no generations were run and no hands-on quality testing was performed. Every quality statement below is either a vendor claim, a third-party anecdote, or an inference, and is labeled as such.
- A Higgsfield MCP connector happened to be attached to this research session. Only its tool schemas were read (via tool discovery); no Higgsfield tool was invoked and no credits were used.
- Engine baseline: Unreal Engine 5.8 was released on 2026-06-17 and the Epic forum announcement lists 5.8.1, 5.8.2 and 5.8.3 hotfix release notes, so 5.8.x is the latest stable line, not 5.7 [Epic forum](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/unreal-engine-5-8-released/2729274); [Unreal Engine news](https://www.unrealengine.com/news/unreal-engine-5-8-is-now-available). 80.lv reports 5.8 is described by Epic as the last major 5.x update [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/unreal-engine-5-8-is-out-today-with-big-optimization-improvements-and-mesh-terrain). Several plugins below still list 5.7 as their newest supported version.
- Evaluation weights used unchanged: output quality 25%, UE compatibility 20%, AI/automation readiness 15%, reliability/maintenance 15%, value 10%, integration complexity 10%, docs/community 5%. Licensing eligibility is treated as a pass/fail gate before scoring, not as a weighted factor.

## 1. Hosted AI 3D generators: Meshy, Tripo, Hyper3D Rodin, Higgsfield 3D, Sloyd, Kaedim, CSM, Luma

### Takeaway
Meshy (paid tiers) has the most complete and best-documented pipeline for an agent-assisted UE workflow in October 2026: an actively maintained official MCP server, an official UE plugin, quad remeshing, rigging, animation and a clear paid-tier commercial license with a no-training-without-consent statement. Hyper3D Rodin is the strongest quality-oriented alternative (quads, baked normals, high-poly) but its API needs the Business tier; Tripo is competitive on price but its Free-tier license wording is internally inconsistent and its MCP is alpha. None of these vendors publishes evidence that outputs are production-ready for hero assets; all of them sell remesh/retopology steps, which is itself an admission that raw output needs processing.

The pricing FAQ conditions ownership on not using materials that violate others' copyrights, and says premium subscribers may sell/distribute outputs [Meshy pricing](https://www.meshy.ai/pricing).
- Free-tier downloads: the help-center comparison table (article dated 2026-08-27) shows "Model Downloads" not included on Free and API access only on Pro and above [Meshy help](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/12062933-which-meshy-plan-is-right-for-you). A search-engine summary of another Meshy help article referred to a capped download allowance for Meshy 6 Lite on Free; this was not fetched directly and conflicts with the table. Treat Free as evaluation only.
- Data/training: "We will NOT share your data or use it for any training purpose without your consent"; web-app data stored on AWS in the United States [Meshy pricing FAQ](https://www.meshy.ai/pricing).
- Models and credits: Meshy 7 and Meshy 6 cost 20 credits per generation (25 with Ultra Mode on Meshy 7); the web-app table lists Remesh, Rigging and Animate at 0 credits [Meshy docs](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/pricing). The official MCP server's README lists API costs of remesh 5, rig 5, animate 3, uv-unwrap 5 credits, so web-app and API pricing differ [meshy-mcp-server README](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server). MCP release v0.6.0 is described as synced with the public Meshy API "as of 2026-10-05" and adds Meshy 7.1 (now "latest" on text-to-3D, image-to-3D, multi-image and refine), a `geometry_resolution` 2k/4k pass (+5 credits), Meshy 6 Lite (5 credits mesh, 2K textures only) and Smart Topology on text-to-3D (`meshy-t2`, 100 to 15,000 faces, triangle only) [meshy-mcp-server releases](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server/releases).

Smart Topology (`meshy-t1`/`meshy-t2`) gives part-separated geometry with a configurable target polycount, and `lowpoly` is deprecated in its favour [meshy-mcp-server README](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server).

- UE integration: free Unreal plugin via "DCC Bridge", one-click send from the web app, animated models supported, Windows binaries for UE 5.4, 5.5, 5.6, 5.7 plus a 5.7 source package; requires a Meshy Pro account or above. UE 5.8 is not listed [Meshy UE plugin page](https://www.meshy.ai/integrations/unreal-engine). Meshy also lists Blender, Unity, 3ds Max, Maya, Godot and an MCP server [Meshy integrations](https://www.meshy.ai/integrations).
- The official MCP server's own built-in guidance for game engines is: generate as FBX, texture, then `meshy_remesh` with quad topology and an appropriate polycount before download [meshy-mcp-server instructions.ts](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server/blob/df531be7/src/instructions.ts).

### Inferences
- Paid tier is mandatory for shipping with Meshy, Tripo, Rodin and Sloyd; free tiers are evaluation-only (CC BY attribution, public gallery, non-commercial, personal-use or no downloads).
- Meshy's lead is in documented automation surface (MCP + API + UE plugin + remesh/rig/animate), not proven visual superiority. Its UE plugin not yet listing 5.8 means a 5.8 project should rely on FBX import or wait for an update.
- Because there is no trustworthy public benchmark, the project should run its own 20-asset bake-off (same reference images, wireframe and UV inspection, Blender 3D Print Toolbox checks, time-to-game-ready) before committing budget. This is cheap relative to a year of subscriptions.
- Higgsfield is primarily a 2D/video tool with a convenience 3D layer; it adds value for concept art, references and look development, while its 3D output should be evaluated like any single-image generator (hallucinated hidden sides). Its default training-on-uploads clause makes it unsuitable for confidential concept art unless on Enterprise.

### Gaps
- Official Rodin, Higgsfield and Tripo Max/Team USD prices; Tripo's full Terms text; whether Tripo or Rodin train on uploaded inputs (not found).
- Underlying model behind Higgsfield `image_to_3d`.
- Current status and terms of CSM Cube and Luma Genie.
- No independent, reproducible topology/UV benchmark exists in the sources found.

## 2. Open-weight 3D generation models: Hunyuan3D, TRELLIS.2, Stability SF3D/SPAR3D, Meta SAM 3D Objects

### Takeaway
Open weights remove per-generation cost and keep data local, but licenses differ sharply. Microsoft TRELLIS.2 (MIT) is the cleanest license for local experimentation, subject to checking its renderer dependencies. Tencent Hunyuan3D open releases fail the commercial-Steam gate for a globally sold game because the community license excludes the EU, UK and South Korea for outputs too, caps MAU and requires machine-generated-content disclosure. Stability SF3D/SPAR3D are usable under the Community License only while revenue stays under $1M. SAM 3D Objects' license appears permissive but needs legal review of its acceptable-use terms.

### Cited Findings
- Hunyuan3D 2.1 Community License (release date June 13, 2025): "THIS LICENSE AGREEMENT DOES NOT APPLY IN THE EUROPEAN UNION, UNITED KINGDOM AND SOUTH KOREA"; Territory = worldwide excluding those [Hugging Face LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hunyuan3D-2.1/blob/main/LICENSE); [GitHub LICENSE](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1/blob/main/LICENSE).
- Same license, fetched text: Section 5(c) prohibits using, reproducing, modifying, distributing or displaying "the Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.1 Works, Output or results" outside the Territory; Section 4 requires a separate license if MAU of all products exceeds 1 million; Section 5(b) forbids using Output to improve any other AI model; Section 6(d) "Tencent claims no rights in Outputs"; Acceptable Use Policy item 12 prohibits publishing content "without expressly and conspicuously identifying that the information and/or content is machine generated" [raw LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1/main/LICENSE). The 2.0 license has the same territory clause [Hunyuan3D-2 LICENSE](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2/blob/main/LICENSE).
- Contradiction: a Tencent Cloud "techpedia" article describes the open-source release as having "a permissive commercial license that lets studios ship products" [Tencent Cloud techpedia](https://www.tencentcloud.com/techpedia/146851); the LICENSE file is authoritative and contradicts this. Another Tencent Cloud page says the hosted API (Tencent HY 3D Global) has separate service terms [Tencent Cloud techpedia 148273](https://www.tencentcloud.com/techpedia/148273?lang=en); those API terms were not reviewed (UNVERIFIED).
- Versions (secondary sources, consistent with each other): newest open weights are 2.1 (June 2025, 3.3B shape + 2B paint, about 29 GB VRAM for the full pipeline) plus Hunyuan3D-Omni and Hunyuan3D-Part (September 2025, same community license); 2.5, PolyGen, 3.0 (announced 2025-09-16) and 3.1 (2026-01-28, up to 8 input views) are hosted-only with no weights [TripoSR blog, secondary](https://triposr.org/blog/hunyuan3d-versions); [Cinevva timeline, secondary](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/ai-3d-model-generation-timeline-2026).
- TRELLIS.2: MIT license for model and code; 4B parameters; image-to-3D with "complex topologies, sharp features, and full PBR materials"; repo created 2025-11-26; training code released; dependencies nvdiffrast and nvdiffrec "governed by" their own licenses [microsoft/TRELLIS.2](https://github.com/microsoft/trellis.2); [Hugging Face model card](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B/blob/main/README.md). Up to 1536³ resolution; handles "open surfaces, non-manifold geometry, fully enclosed volumes"; trained on 500K assets; described as "purely a research project" [TRELLIS.2 project page](https://microsoft.github.io/TRELLIS.2/); [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/workbench/project/trellis).
- Stability SF3D and SPAR3D: Stability AI Community License, free for research, non-commercial and commercial use under US$1M annual revenue; commercial users must register; above $1M an Enterprise license is required "before commercially using SF3D or any derivative work of SF3D or its outputs" [SF3D model card](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-fast-3d/blob/main/README.md); [SPAR3D LICENSE](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-point-aware-3d/blob/main/LICENSE.md); [Stability license page](https://stability.ai/license).
- Meta SAM 3D Objects: checkpoints launched 2025-11-19, encoder weights 2026-06-01, licensed under the "SAM License" [sam-3d-objects repo](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-objects). The license grants a worldwide royalty-free right to use, reproduce, distribute and modify, users own their derivatives, with termination on litigation against Meta and an indemnity [SAM License](https://huggingface.co/facebook/sam-3d-objects/blob/main/LICENSE).
- MCP for Blender supports a "Local API mode" for self-hosted Hunyuan3D [DeepWiki on blender-mcp, secondary](https://deepwiki.com/ahujasid/blender-mcp/3.4-hunyuan3d-integration).

### Inferences
- A Steam game is sold and displayed in the EU, UK and South Korea, so shipping Hunyuan3D-derived meshes conflicts with Section 5(c) as written; this is a gate failure, not a trade-off, unless Tencent grants a separate license or counsel concludes otherwise. Developers located in those territories cannot use it at all.
- TRELLIS.2's "arbitrary topology / non-manifold" capability is good for appearance but means outputs need watertight/manifold repair before collision, baking or retopology.
- Open models are best used as local, private ideation and blockout tools on a 24 GB+ GPU (hardware specifics UNVERIFIED for TRELLIS.2), not as a shipping pipeline, until the project has its own legal review.

### Gaps
- nvdiffrast/nvdiffrec license terms (historically NVIDIA source licenses with possible non-commercial clauses; UNVERIFIED here) and whether they matter when only inference outputs are shipped.
- TRELLIS.2 VRAM and runtime requirements.
- SAM License acceptable-use exhibit, if any.
- Tencent Cloud Hunyuan 3D API output terms and territory restrictions.

## 3. Photogrammetry and Gaussian splatting: RealityScan (desktop and Mobile), Polycam, KIRI Engine, UE splat plugins

### Takeaway
Epic's RealityScan desktop (formerly RealityCapture) is the clear first choice: free for entities under $1M trailing-12-month revenue, measured rather than hallucinated geometry, and since 2.1 a documented CLI plus REST/gRPC automation with Python samples. Phone apps (RealityScan Mobile, Polycam, KIRI) are fine for quick captures, but Polycam's default AI-training setting and broad content license matter for unreleased IP. Gaussian splats now render in UE 5.8 via third-party plugins, but they remain unsuited as playable, collidable, relightable gameplay geometry.

### Cited Findings
- Naming and licensing: RealityCapture was renamed RealityScan with RealityScan 2.0 (June 2025); the phone app became RealityScan Mobile [RealityScan 2.0 news](https://www.realityscan.com/news/realityscan-20-new-release-brings-powerful-new-features-to-a-rebranded-realitycapture). Free for individuals and companies under $1M USD revenue in the past 12 months, educators and students; above that, $1,250 per seat per year [RealityScan licensing](https://www.realityscan.com/license). The EULA counts the whole corporate group and includes "advances received or other funds raised" in revenue; there is also a non-commercial exception [RealityScan EULA](https://www.realityscan.com/eula).
- RealityScan 2.0 added AI-powered background masking, better alignment and a Quality Analysis tool [CG Channel](https://www.cgchannel.com/2025/06/epic-games-releases-realityscan-2-0-and-realityscan-mobile-1-7/).
- RealityScan 2.1 (2025-11-20): CLI reference documentation, templating (replacing reporting), REST and gRPC plugins with Python samples and "full Linux support", SLAM and classified LAS/LAZ import, UV checker map, render-from-camera, OpenCV registration export [RealityScan 2.1 news](https://www.realityscan.com/news/realityscan-2-1-is-now-available). The Linux CLI build runs through a bundled Wine environment; texture defragmentation enabled by default [Digital Production](https://digitalproduction.com/2025/11/27/epic-games-updates-realityscan-2-1-out/). 2.1.1 (reported 2026-04-03) added XMP export options and expanded COLMAP support [Toolfarm](https://www.toolfarm.com/news/realityscan-2-1-1/).

## 7. Workflow analysis: six pipelines, quality criteria, and when not to use AI

### Takeaway
AI 3D is a fast way to produce blockouts and background clutter, not finished hero assets. Every workflow converges on the same human-and-tool cleanup stage (manifold repair, retopology, UVs, baking, PBR rebuild, LODs/Nanite, collision). Photogrammetry and purchased scans beat AI wherever real-world plausibility, dimensional accuracy or close-up inspection matter, which describes most of a photoreal urban espionage game.

### Cited Findings
- Vendors' own pipelines include post-generation remesh/retopology steps (Meshy Remesh to quads, Tripo retopology in its paid toolkit, Rodin Smart Low-Poly and baked normals), confirming raw output is not final [Meshy help](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/9992029-how-to-download-a-quad-mesh-model); [Tripo pricing](https://www.tripo3d.ai/pricing); [Hyper3D pricing](https://hyper3d.ai/pricing).
- Single-image limits: Higgsfield's tool contract states the mesh "reproduces only what is in the source image" and recommends editing the image first to add or change content [Higgsfield MCP schema](https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp); Higgsfield describes `multi_image_to_3d` as better geometric accuracy than single image (same source).
- Multi-view inputs: Meshy multi-image (up to 4 views), Rodin 1 to 5 images, Hunyuan 3.1 up to 8 views (secondary), Higgsfield 2 to 4 views [meshy-mcp-server](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server/releases); [Rodin Gen-2 docs](https://docs.hyper3d.ai/en/api-specification/rodin-gen2); [Cinevva, secondary](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/ai-3d-model-generation-timeline-2026).
- Auto-rigging claims: Meshy rig + 600+ animation presets on paid plans [Meshy help](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/12062933-which-meshy-plan-is-right-for-you); KIRI "Auto Rig Generation" on Pro [KIRI pricing](https://www.kiriengine.app/pricing); Sloyd "AI rigging and animation" [Sloyd pricing](https://www.sloyd.ai/pricing); Higgsfield `3d_rigging` [Higgsfield MCP schema](https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp). No independent test of deformation quality was found; one review reports auto-rig struggles on chunky proportions and that hero faces/hands break [meshyiai.com, Low confidence](https://meshyiai.com/review/).
- TRELLIS.2 explicitly generates non-manifold/open surfaces [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/workbench/project/trellis); RealityScan Mobile 1.9 now defaults to watertight meshes in Object mode [RealityScan Mobile 1.9](https://www.realityscan.com/release/realityscan-mobile-19-is-now-available).
- RealityScan 2.1 improved UV unwrapping (checker map, defragmentation) relevant to scan cleanup [Digital Production](https://digitalproduction.com/2025/11/27/epic-games-updates-realityscan-2-1-out/).

### Inferences

Workflow (1) Single image to 3D mesh
- Tools: Meshy 7.1, Rodin Gen-2/2.5, Tripo v3.x, TRELLIS.2 (local), Higgsfield `image_to_3d`/`sam_3_3d`.
- Expect: convincing front view; invented back/underside ("back-side hallucination"); lighting baked into albedo; fused parts (a lamp and its base as one blob); dense triangle soup or vendor remesh; fragmented UVs.
- Use for: background clutter, blockout, kitbash fodder. Feed it clean, evenly lit, orthographic-style concept images you own.

Workflow (2) Multiple images to 3D
- Two different things share this name. (a) AI multi-view (2 to 8 views): improves silhouette and back sides but is still generative and can drift between views. (b) True photogrammetry (dozens to hundreds of overlapping photos in RealityScan): measured geometry. For real objects, always prefer (b).

Workflow (3) Text to textured 3D
- Least controllable; style drift across a set. Better pattern: text to image (iterate cheaply in 2D with art direction), approve the image, then image/multi-view to 3D. Meshy's text-to-3D and Smart Topology text mode exist but are best for placeholder props.

Workflow (4) Smartphone photos to photogrammetry
- Capture: overcast or shaded light, 60 to 80% overlap, locked exposure (RealityScan Mobile has manual camera mode), scale reference. Process in RealityScan desktop for control (AI masking, alignment QA) or the phone app's cloud for speed.
- Legal hygiene: avoid trademarks, logos, licence plates, recognizable people, and copyrighted artworks/architecture where local law restricts; KIRI notes scanned proprietary objects may be copyrighted [KIRI FAQ](https://www.kiriengine.app/faq/do-i-own-the-3d-scans-i-create).

Workflow (5) AI base mesh to Blender refinement to UE
- Generate (FBX) then in Blender via MCP for Blender: merge by distance, recalc normals, fix non-manifold, separate parts, scale to real-world units, set pivot; retopologize (vendor quad remesh, Quad Remesher or manual for hero items); UV unwrap and pack; bake high-to-low normals/AO; rebuild or correct materials to the project's master material in Substance (specialist 04); create LODs or enable Nanite for static meshes (specialist 09); author simple collision; export FBX; import via UE Interchange; validate in a lighting test map.
- Agent role: Claude Code can drive generation, Blender cleanup scripts, naming, LOD/collision setup and batch import; a human must approve silhouettes, scale, materials and art consistency.

Workflow (6) Scanned mesh to retopology to optimized playable asset
- RealityScan high-poly (millions of tris) then simplify; retopo or decimate for low poly; unwrap; bake normals/AO/curvature from high poly; delight albedo; derive roughness; make tileable where needed; LODs or Nanite; collision; UE material instance. RealityScan CLI makes the reconstruction stage batchable.

Quality criteria checklist (apply to every candidate asset; "looks good in one screenshot" is not acceptance)
- Visual fidelity at gameplay camera distance under neutral and harsh lighting; geometry accuracy versus reference and real-world scale; back-side and underside inspection; watertight/manifold status; topology (quad-dominant where it will deform or be edited; triangle count budget); UV utilization, seams and texel density; PBR consistency (no baked shadows in albedo, plausible roughness/metal); part separation for doors/lids/moving parts; deformation test after auto-rig; LOD transitions; collision; naming and pivots.

When traditional modeling or buying scanned assets is preferable
- Buy or hand-model (preferred over AI): hero props and gadgets seen up close in third person (weapons, phones, keycards, briefcases), anything interactive with moving parts, modular architecture kits that must snap to a grid and use trim sheets, vehicles (specialist 07), characters and faces (specialist 02), signage and branded-looking items (to control IP), and key art.
- Buy scans (Fab/Megascans-type libraries, specialist 01) or scan yourself: ground materials, rocks, debris, vegetation, street furniture, weathered surfaces, which matches photoreal benchmarks better than generative output.
- Use AI 3D: background clutter on shelves and rooftops, distant set dressing, greybox/blockout, quick variety passes, prototype vertical slices, previs, and look-dev, then replace or upgrade anything that becomes prominent.

### Gaps
- No measured time-to-game-ready data per tool; the project should log its own times during the proposed bake-off.
