# Specialist 06: Open Worlds, Cities, and Procedural Environments (UE5, solo/small team, commercial Steam release)

Research date and access date for every source below: 2026-10-09. Scope: procedural environment generation for an original photorealistic third-person espionage/urban action game. Overlaps (assets/marketplaces 01, characters/MetaHuman 02, vehicles 07, gameplay AI 08, rendering/perf 09, MCP depth 11, licensing 12) are mentioned only briefly.

Method notes for the report writer:
- Many dev.epicgames.com documentation pages and fab.com pages returned HTTP 403 or DNS failures to direct fetches; content was obtained through a search/crawl service (Exa) that returned page text or highlights. Where only a secondary source summarized an Epic doc, this is stated.
- Several search results were piracy or "free download" mirrors of paid Fab products (ueassetsfree, renderskill, desirefx, assetsue, yunqiaonet, shop-assets3d). They were NOT used as sources and must never be used to obtain content.
- StraySpark blog posts (2026) appeared repeatedly; spot checks found inaccuracies (it calls MassTraffic "built-in" although it ships as a City Sample project plugin, and its Megaplants species table does not match the Fab Megaplants listings). Treat StraySpark as unreliable; it is cited only where explicitly flagged.

## KQ1. Findings per tool/topic, with evidence and URLs

### Takeaway
As of 2026-10-09 the latest stable engine is UE 5.8 (5.8.2 hotfix), the final planned UE5 release. The biggest change for this project is that Epic's free City Sample was updated on 2026-08-27 with a city rebuilt entirely in UE using PCG (production-ready since 5.7) plus an Experimental Unreal MCP plugin that lets an LLM drive the PCG graphs; however, the new PCG city ships without the original level's Mass traffic, pedestrians and traffic lights, and it leans on Experimental/Beta systems (Nanite Foliage, Procedural Vegetation Editor, Megaplants, MCP) that Epic itself says should not be shipped.

#### B. City Sample (current availability, version, contents, license)
- Availability: free on Fab, the Unreal Editor Home Panel and the Epic Games Launcher; updated for UE 5.8 on 2026-08-27. [Epic: City Sample gets a major update with PCG and Unreal MCP workflows](https://www.unrealengine.com/learning/city-sample-gets-a-major-update-with-pcg-and-unreal-mcp-workflows); [Fab listing: City Sample](https://www.fab.com/listings/4898e707-7855-404b-af0e-a505ee690e68)
- Fab listing text (crawl dated 2026-08-27): "With 5.8, we added our building primitives and examples that allow LLMs to build cities using the Unreal MCP server. Note this version of City Sample is built entirely in-engine using the PCG framework. You can find these examples and building primitives in the CitySamplePCG plugin." Separate extracted packs remain listed: City Sample Buildings, City Sample Vehicles, City Sample Crowd. [Fab listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/4898e707-7855-404b-af0e-a505ee690e68); [legacy Marketplace page](https://www.unrealengine.com/marketplace/en-US/product/city-sample)
- What the 2026 update contains (Epic): the original Houdini-built level, plus two new PCG levels built "with assistance from the LLM" (a full city and a simpler example level); multiple PCG graph examples usable as baseline references for the Unreal MCP server; five Megaplants species; PCG Assembly data assets (rooftop plazas, piers); several shape grammar definition assets for buildings and roads; a PCG Primitive subgraph for City Sample buildings; the CitySamplePCG_demo plugin with all graphs/assets. Epic: "The sample relies on several Experimental and Beta systems, some of which are not enabled by default." Epic states it used Claude for the LLM-assisted build. [Epic news](https://www.unrealengine.com/learning/city-sample-gets-a-major-update-with-pcg-and-unreal-mcp-workflows)
- Limitations of the new PCG city (secondary source summarizing Epic's City Sample PCG docs, which returned 403 to direct fetch): the new main demo lacks the original level's Mass AI traffic and pedestrians and has no traffic-light system; its basic mode lets you walk and fly; it starts with World Partition streaming disabled and generated content unpartitioned; the main city uses 18 interdependent graphs and Epic recommends pausing generation while editing. [ZerotoBeast, 2026-09-13](https://zerotobeast.com/unreal-engine-5-8-city-sample-a-free-pcg-worldbuilding-lab/). Primary doc URL (403 to fetch): [City Sample PCG for Unreal Engine](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/city-sample-pcg-for-unreal-engine)
- A YouTube walkthrough of the PCG Primitives content (engine plugin "PCG primitives and examples library for world building", enabled under Plugins, content under Engine Plugins) shows the graph chain: city shape spline (arteries) -> districts -> lots -> ground (concrete lots, sidewalks) -> building generator (modular wall/window pieces) -> leftover lots (trees/parks) -> city contour. The creator reports road misalignment in an early version and that he could not reproduce Epic's demo results with the same prompts via MCP (anecdotal, single user). [Smart Poly YouTube: UE 5.8 PCG City Generator Sample Project](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR-_DXy6vFc)
- Original City Sample (Houdini level) per Epic docs: Big City about 4 km x 4 km (audio section says 16 square km), Small City for all features; systems: World Partition + OFPA, Data Layers, Nanite on all static meshes (no traditional LODs), Lumen with Hardware Ray Tracing, Virtual Shadow Maps, Mass AI traffic and crowds based on the MetaHuman library, Chaos vehicles and destruction, MetaSounds, Niagara; playable walk/drive/fly with in-game density sliders for crowd, traffic and parked cars. [Epic doc: City Sample Project](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/city-sample-project-unreal-engine-demonstration)
- How the original city was generated: entirely in Houdini with custom tools; Houdini exports a huge point cloud; the Rule Processor (tool built for this project) converts point clouds into thousands of instances; buildings are built from volumes styled by a shape grammar, with bottom/top split styles; "Each building in the city is made up of hundreds of instances"; very little custom geometry (custom meshes for collisions, freeway deck, road decals, custom roofs). The Houdini source files ship as CitySample_HoudiniFiles.zip and "You will need a license from SideFX in order to use the City Building Generator asset". [Epic doc: City Sample Project](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/city-sample-project-unreal-engine-demonstration); [Epic doc: Generating a City and Freeway in UE5](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/city-sample-quick-start-for-generating-a-city-and-freeway-in-unreal-engine-5)
- City Sample buildings are exteriors with faked interiors: Epic's State of Unreal 2022 talk describes window interiors built with interior mapping plus a parallax "3D print method", with per-window room data packed into a 32-bit unsigned integer stored as Per Instance Custom Data on building ISMs. [Epic, The Matrix Awakens: Creating a World, SoU 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xLVJP-o0g28)
- Recommended spec for the City Sample: Windows 10 DX12, 12-core 3.4 GHz CPU, 64 GB RAM, RTX 2080 / Radeon 6000 or higher, 8 GB VRAM, SSD. [Epic doc: City Sample Project](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/city-sample-project-unreal-engine-demonstration)
- License: Epic's Content EULA defines "UE-Only Content" (Licensed Content designated for use only with Unreal Engine-based products), forbids selling Licensed Content "on a stand-alone basis", and restricts "NoAI Content" from generative-AI datasets/inputs. Projects must "reasonably add value beyond the value of the Licensed Content". [Unreal Engine Content EULA](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/content). A 2024 forum post quotes the City Sample listing as "UE-Only Content - Licensed for Use Only with Unreal Engine-based Products"; ZerotoBeast (2026-09-13) says the live Fab listing links the Fab Standard License. The Fab Standard License summary permits commercial use and distributing Projects incorporating the assets. [Forum: FAB UE-only content licensing](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/fab-ue-only-content-licensing/2082870); [Fab EULA](https://www.fab.com/eula); [ZerotoBeast](https://zerotobeast.com/unreal-engine-5-8-city-sample-a-free-pcg-worldbuilding-lab/). Disagreement: which exact license text applies to the 5.8 listing was not directly readable (fab.com blocked); both readings allow shipping a commercial UE game containing the content; neither allows reselling the assets standalone or use in another engine (UE-Only reading).

#### C. PCG framework (status, Biome, GPU, runtime)
- PCG was declared Production-Ready in UE 5.7 (Epic announcement dated 2025-11-12); Epic said PCG is almost twice as fast as in 5.5, GPU compute was optimized for multiple platforms including FastGeo support, GPU node parameter overrides were added, and a new PCG Editor Mode provides spline drawing, point painting and volume tools. [Epic news: UE 5.7 is now available](https://www2.unrealengine.com/news/unreal-engine-5-7-is-now-available); [GameFromScratch: UE 5.7 Released](https://gamefromscratch.com/unreal-engine-5-7-released/)
- Source disagreement: SideFX's Houdini Engine v3.0.7 release notes (H21 era) still said "PCG itself is still considered experimental by Epic"; SideFX's newer H22 docs say "As PCG is no longer considered an experimental plugin by Epic since the release of UE5.7, we no longer provide a separate ... plugin". Epic's own 5.7 announcement is authoritative: PCG core is production-ready. [HoudiniEngineForUnreal v3.0.7](https://github.com/sideeffects/HoudiniEngineForUnreal/releases/tag/v3.0.7); [SideFX: Introduction to Houdini Engine for Unreal](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/unreal/intro)
- PCG in 5.8: nondestructive manual edits on top of generated content, complex attribute types, embedded subgraphs, new spatial example graphs. [Epic news: UE 5.8](https://www.unrealengine.com/news/unreal-engine-5-8-is-now-available); [Epic forum: UE 5.8 Preview](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/unreal-engine-5-8-preview/2721597)
- PCG basics: PCG Component on an actor or PCG Volume; works in editor and at runtime; node categories include Hierarchical Generation, Spawner, Sampler, Spatial, IO (Alembic and external data). The Geometry Script Interop plugin is needed to sample points on static meshes. [Epic doc: PCG Overview (5.8)](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/procedural-content-generation-overview)
- PCG Biome Core and PCG Biome Sample: added in 5.5, still v0.2 Experimental in 5.6, 5.7 and 5.8 (third-party plugin tracker). [Unreal Directive: PCG Biome Core](https://unrealdirective.com/resources/engine-plugins/pcgbiomecore/); [Unreal Directive: PCG Biome Sample](https://unrealdirective.com/resources/engine-plugins/pcgbiomesample/). An Epic staff reply (2025-09-29) says partitioning of local biomes is not supported "before at least 5.8" and runtime biome core may not work with a partitioned global core; advice is to use smaller/multiple biomes. [Forum: PCG Biomes v2 partitioning](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/pcg-biomes-v2-partitioning-for-local-biome-graphs/2680350)
- Runtime PCG cost evidence: a studio profiling PS5 runtime hierarchical generation (grass/rocks, GPU spawn, precomputed point cloud) saw ~1 ms spikes against a 500 microsecond CPU budget after 5.7 improvements; Epic staff recommended disabling the cache for pure-runtime cases and pointed to 5.8 runtime scheduler/GPU work and the PCG FastGeo Interop plugin, which "removes the need for components & actors to support the runtime GPU generation". [Forum: Runtime Cost in PCG Hierarchical Generation](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/runtime-cost-in-pcg-hierarchical-generation/2708776)
- PCGEx (PCG Extended Toolkit): MIT licensed, "200+ nodes" adding graph theory (Delaunay, Voronoi, MST, convex hull), A*/Dijkstra pathfinding, path ops, spatial queries; badges for UE 5.3 to 5.8; available free on Fab or from GitHub; 667 stars, primarily one maintainer (Nebukam, 4,443 contributions). [GitHub: PCGExtendedToolkit](https://github.com/Nebukam/PCGExtendedToolkit); [PCGEx docs: About](https://pcgex.gitbook.io/pcgex/infos/about)

#### D. Unreal MCP for procedural generation (brief; specialist 11 owns depth)
- Epic's MCP plugin is Experimental in 5.8; it lets "any model you want" connect to the engine and project, with built-in exposure to Blueprints, assets, levels, materials, meshes and more, and is extensible. [Epic news: UE 5.8](https://www.unrealengine.com/news/unreal-engine-5-8-is-now-available)
- Epic doc on City Sample PCG + MCP: the PCG primitive framework is "designed to work with an MCP server"; an LLM uses a "PCG graph generation skill" and a "shape grammar definition skill". Basic prompts that work: restrict to older building styles (tooltips describe styles, type, approximate construction date), clamp building height to 200 m, swap vegetation for tropical species (only if such assets exist in the project), set lighting for a place/time, double road widths (local, collector, arteries), add parking. Advanced prompts (add a district category; remove all parked cars, which requires editing shape grammar or duplicating a sidewalk) need "a deeper understanding of how the PCG graph works in order to check the quality of the LLM's work." [Epic doc: City Sample PCG and MCP Server Interaction](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/city-sample-pcg-and-mcp-server-interaction-in-unreal-engine)
- A third-party reference of the official MCP's PCGToolset (UE 5.8.0) lists tools such as CreateGraph, AddNode, AddSubgraphNode, ConnectNodePins, DrawSpline, ExecuteGraphInstance, SpawnGraphInstance, Get/SetGraphInstanceParams, GetGraphSchema, ListNativeNodes. Secondary source; not verified against Epic docs. [tc-imba: UE Official MCP PCGToolset reference](https://tc-imba.github.io/ue-official-mcp/references/toolsets/PCGToolset.PCGToolset/)
- Secondary summary of Epic's MCP docs: plugin labeled Experimental, APIs can change, local connection, the documented server "has no authentication layer and is not safe to expose beyond the local machine." [ZerotoBeast](https://zerotobeast.com/unreal-engine-5-8-city-sample-a-free-pcg-worldbuilding-lab/)
- Community harness (Claude Code + official Unreal MCP on UE 5.8) reports practical constraints: one editor/one game thread, so "serialize every scene mutation"; viewport captures are multi-MB base64 and must be decoded outside the agent context; it also demonstrates streaming real NYC via Cesium + Google 3D Tiles for flythroughs (demo use, not shipping). [GitHub: per-simmons/unreal-agent-harness](https://github.com/per-simmons/unreal-agent-harness)

#### E. World Partition, level streaming, HLOD, Fast Geometry Streaming
- World Partition stores the world in a single persistent level split into grid cells streamed by distance from streaming sources (player controllers, or World Partition Streaming Source components, e.g. pre-loading a teleport target). HLODs are built via Build > World Partition or the WorldPartitionHLODsBuilder commandlet; builder commandlets also generate nav data and batch-resave actors without loading the whole world. [Epic doc: World Partition (5.8)](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/world-partition-in-unreal-engine)
- City Sample uses World Partition with Data Layers (no sublevels) and HLOD generation is part of Epic's city quick start. [Epic doc: City Sample Project](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/city-sample-project-unreal-engine-demonstration); [Epic doc: Generating a City and Freeway](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/city-sample-quick-start-for-generating-a-city-and-freeway-in-unreal-engine-5)
- Fast Geometry Streaming plugin (used in The Witcher 4 UE5 tech demo) remains Experimental in 5.8, "rapidly load[s] static, non-gameplay assets for massive, seamless open worlds". [Epic news: UE 5.8](https://www.unrealengine.com/news/unreal-engine-5-8-is-now-available)
- GeoScape docs give a practical recipe consistent with Epic's tooling: build into a normal level, run WorldPartitionConvertCommandlet and WorldPartitionHLODsBuilder, keep the Landscape always loaded so distant terrain stays visible. [GeoScape docs](https://geoscape.unrealtools.com/docs)

#### H. Vegetation: Megaplants, PVE, Nanite Foliage, SpeedTree
- Megaplants are free on Fab under the Fab Standard License; existing Megaplants were converted for PVE in 5.8 and "Going forward, Megaplants released will be compatible with UE 5.8 and above only." [Quixel news, 2026-07-16](https://quixel.com/news/discover-the-latest-quixel-megascans-and-free-megaplants); [Quixel news, 2026-04-08](https://quixel.com/news/quixel-on-fab-new-megascans-and-megaplants). The Fab seller page showed 33 results at crawl time. [Fab: Quixel Megaplants seller](https://www.fab.com/sellers/Quixel%20Megaplants)
- A Megaplants Fab listing requires enabling the PVE plugin and "Nanite Foliage (Experimental)" and states: "Megaplants and the Procedural Vegetation Editor are currently Experimental ... We do not recommend shipping projects with Experimental features." [Fab: Megaplants Norway Maple Forest](https://www.fab.com/listings/e789ad04-cf79-4973-adb0-85c794d77144)
- PVE (5.8): node-based vegetation creation built on PCG; outputs Nanite Foliage assets (instancing, GPU skeletal-driven animation, voxel representation at distance), compatible with Dynamic Wind; new botanical Grower; "5.7 assets are not compatible with 5.8"; "PVE is experimental. Substantial architectural changes can occur between releases." [Epic doc: Procedural Vegetation Editor](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/procedural-vegetation-editor-in-unreal-engine)
- Shipping risk evidence: users reported that PVE/Megaplants meshes crash packaged builds in 5.8 and 5.8.1 (work in editor); bug UE-386321 marked Fixed with target 5.8.2. Whether 5.8.2 resolves all cases was not confirmed in the sources read. [Forum: PVE Nanite Foliage meshes crash packaged project](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/procedural-vegetation-nanite-foliage-meshes-crash-packaged-project/2730886). 5.8 Preview users also reported broken PVE samples and non-Nanite export problems. [Forum: UE 5.8 Preview](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/unreal-engine-5-8-preview/2721597)
- SpeedTree: owned and sold by Unity; tiers: Indie $19/month (revenue/funding under $100K), Pro $899/year (under $1M), Library add-on $999/year, Enterprise by quote (over $1M); Internet access required for Indie/Pro; 30-day limited-export trial; logo use must carry "© [year] Unity Technologies". [Unity: SpeedTree](https://unity.com/products/speedtree); [SpeedTree Store](https://store.speedtree.com/store/speedtree-games-indie-9)
- SpeedTree in UE: .st9 import supported from UE 4.27 (SpeedTree 9/10+), works with Nanite since UE 5.1. [Unity docs: Importing into Unreal (ST9)](https://docs.unity3d.com/speedtree-modeler/manual/import-to-unreal.html); [Epic doc: Using SpeedTree](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/using-speedtree-in-unreal-engine). But Epic staff say SpeedTree formats are "unsuitable to represent Nanite Assemblies" (needed for Nanite Foliage) and there are no current plans to collaborate; CD Projekt Red wrote a custom importer for the Witcher 4 demo. ### Inferences
- The City Sample is now two different things: (a) a proven, fully simulated Houdini-built city (traffic, crowds, parked cars, drivable vehicles) whose generation pipeline requires Houdini and whose AI pieces are hard to transplant, and (b) a new PCG/MCP-editable city that is a better learning base for in-engine procedural cities but is not a living world and is not shipping-safe out of the box (Experimental vegetation, streaming disabled by default, no traffic lights).
- PCG core is safe to build on; the risky parts are the satellites (PVE, Nanite Foliage, Megaplants, Biome, MCP, Mesh Terrain, FastGeo, Water, Mass). A solo dev should bake PCG output (static instances with HLODs) for the city and keep runtime PCG to small, budgeted scatter.
- For a city game, external terrain generators (Gaea, World Creator) are secondary: urban ground is mostly roads, sidewalks and lots. They matter only for surrounding hills, parks or a coastline.
- Houdini Indie is the strongest generator for production-grade city logic, but its $100K revenue cap means a commercially successful game forces a move to commercial Houdini (price not published for Core/FX in the sources read); baked outputs in UE do not require Houdini at runtime.
- Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles fail the shipping gate for this project (no caching/baking, no extraction for collision/nav, per-player API calls on the developer's billing, daily root-request cap, mandatory on-screen attribution). OSM is viable with attribution, but raw OSM geometry is a layout input, not finished art.

## KQ2. Topic-specific analysis: environment generation vs living world, per-deliverable plan, variation/consistency/performance, solo scope

### Takeaway
Procedural tools can produce a believable photoreal district for one person, but only from a small number of coherent modular kits plus heavy manual art direction at street level; everything that makes a city feel alive for an espionage game (traffic, pedestrians, navigation, police, missions, enterable interiors) is a separate engineering effort that the City Sample only partially demonstrates and that the new PCG City Sample does not include at all. Recommended scope: a Hitman-style dense sandbox of a few city blocks per mission area, not a GTA-scale open city.

### Cited Findings
- Epic positions the new City Sample as "no black boxes or magic bullets"; its own MCP doc warns that advanced LLM edits require deep graph understanding to check quality. ### Inferences

#### 2.1 Procedural ENVIRONMENT generation vs LIVING-WORLD SIMULATION (explicit separation)
| Layer | What procedural environment tools give you | What they do NOT give you (must be built or bought separately; mostly specialists 07/08) |
|---|---|---|
| Static world | Road network, lots, building shells, sidewalks, street furniture, vegetation, HLODs | Gameplay-correct collision everywhere, cover placement, sightlines for stealth, mission-specific layouts |
| Navigation | Possibly ZoneGraph lanes if the generator exports them (City Sample Houdini pipeline, MetaRoad, HoudiniMassTranslator) | NavMesh tuning for player-relevant AI, door/stair links, interiors, chase paths |
| Traffic | Lane data and spawn points at best | Vehicle AI, intersections and signal logic, reaction to player, pursuit/police, collisions with physics vehicles (City Sample traffic is a project plugin, crowds/traffic built on Experimental Mass) |
| Pedestrians | Sidewalk lanes and spawn points | Crowd behavior, reactions (panic, witnesses, suspicion), LOD from Mass entities to full actors |
| Interactivity | Nothing beyond static meshes | Enterable interiors, doors, NPC schedules, Smart Objects, missions, save state |

Practical conclusion: budget the living world as its own milestone. The original City Sample's MassTraffic and crowd code are a reference implementation, reusable only by someone comfortable porting C++ project plugins across engine versions (forum migration difficulty; fork exists for 5.7). Mass plugins are Experimental in 5.8 and their API changed in 5.8, so lock the engine version early.

#### 2.3 Variation, consistency, texture reuse, performance, art direction
- Variation: combine seed-driven massing (shape grammar), material parameter variation (paint, dirt, signage), per-instance custom data (window states, lights), and decals; avoid relying on many unique textures.
- Consistency: pick one architectural era/region per district and one or two kits per district; mixed marketplace kits with different texel density, trim conventions and color grading are the most common way procedural cities look fake.
- Texture reuse: trim sheets and tiling materials with macro variation; Virtual Texturing for roads (several road tools require VT); keep unique textures for hero landmarks only.
- Performance: Nanite instancing keeps geometry cost manageable, but instance counts, shadow cost (VSM), lights, and collision still need budgets; bake PCG output in editor, build HLODs, and test packaged builds early on target hardware (specialist 09).
- Manual art direction: plan for hand passes on composition (landmarks, sightlines, skyline), street-level storytelling, and mission spaces. PCG 5.8 manual overrides reduce rework when regenerating.

#### 2.4 Realistic scope recommendation for a solo developer (inference)
- Target Hitman-style dense sandboxes: 2 to 4 mission districts, each roughly 4 to 12 city blocks of playable street space, with distant city as HLOD/backdrop meshes.
- One fully dressed downtown district plus one residential neighborhood is achievable for a first vertical slice in months, not weeks, using City Sample Buildings/PCG primitives and one commercial kit with interiors.
- Living world: light ambient traffic and pedestrians that do not need deep reactions; build focused AI (guards, police, targets) with standard UE AI (specialist 08). Avoid committing to GTA-like systemic traffic and crowds unless a dedicated engineer can own Mass.
- Engine version: build on UE 5.8.x (final UE5; Houdini Engine, Cesium and PCGEx all support it). Avoid Experimental features in shipping paths unless tested in packaged builds.

### Gaps
- No published benchmarks were found comparing frame time for City Sample PCG vs Houdini levels, or for Megaplants vs SpeedTree trees in a city scene.
- No primary data on how long a solo developer takes to dress one city block to "AAA screenshot" quality; estimates above are judgment.

## KQ3. Category verdicts

### Inferences
- Best overall: UE PCG framework (production-ready since 5.7) with City Sample 5.8 PCG primitives and shape grammar assets as the starting reference, plus PCGEx for road graphs and pathing. Free, native, MCP-addressable, and bakeable. Caveat: the sample's Experimental/Beta dependencies must be stripped or replaced for shipping.
- Best free: same stack (PCG + PCGEx + City Sample Buildings/Vehicles packs + Megascans where licensed). For terrain, Gaea Community/World Creator Community are non-commercial or export-disabled, so they are not "free for shipping".
- Best paid: Houdini Indie (about $299/year, Medium confidence) with free Houdini Engine Indie, for road networks, lane data (ZoneGraph via HoudiniMassTranslator) and building grammars; switch to commercial Houdini if the business exceeds $100K revenue or $1M funding.
- Best AI-assisted: Epic's Unreal MCP plugin driving PCG graphs (Experimental). It is the only AI path with first-party, documented city-generation prompts; use it for parameter edits and graph inspection under source control, not for unsupervised world building.
- Best for an indie/solo dev: PCG + a commercial road tool (Realistic Customizable Roads or Road Creator Pro; choose after checking current Fab version support) + one coherent modular building kit with real interiors for hero buildings + City Sample Buildings for backdrop.
- Not recommended:
  - Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles (directly or via Cesium ion) as shipped game geometry: caching, offline use and geodata extraction are prohibited; overlays may not be derived from the tiles; on-screen Google attribution required; per-project daily root-request cap and per-player billing.
  - Baking Google terrain or tiles into a project with third-party importers (e.g. plugins that layer OSM on Google terrain): likely conflicts with the no-store/no-cache rule; the plugin vendor itself places ToS responsibility on the user.
  - Shipping Megaplants/PVE/Nanite Foliage content without packaged-build testing on 5.8.2 or later: Epic labels them Experimental and advises against shipping Experimental features; packaged-build crash reports exist.
  - Buildify 1.0 for a new UE 5.8 pipeline: pinned to Blender 3.2 and not updated; fine as inspiration only.
  - Any "free download" mirror of paid Fab tools (several appeared in searches): piracy, license violation.
  - Relying on the City Sample's look as-is for a commercial game: legal to ship within UE terms, but highly recognizable; reskin and recompose.

### Gaps
- Scores are not backed by hands-on tests; road tool rankings especially depend on current Fab versions and prices that were not readable.
